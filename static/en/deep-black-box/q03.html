What to take away
Two knobs cannot do it. y = ax + b only ever draws a straight line, so it cannot make a hill. Even the best straight line leaves an error of 14.28. No setting of the knobs gets you to 3.0.
Problem 3 exists so that you can confirm it is out of reach. That is the end of it, so go straight on to Problem 4 below. Add more knobs and you get there.
Where this pays off
